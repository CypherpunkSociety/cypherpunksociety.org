what we do

Privacy and cognitive autonomy require active defense and shared infrastructure. Born in the San Francisco Bay Area in 1992 out of The WELL, Community Memory, and toad.com—and inspired by interdisciplinary spaces like Paris's La Paillasse and La Quadrature du Net (π)—the Cypherpunk Society equips builders, researchers, and investigative minds through hands-on practice:

  • Surveillance Self-Defense, Digital Hygiene & Self-Hosting: Weekly hands-on clinics covering EFF's Surveillance Self-Defense, Andrej Karpathy's Digital Hygiene baseline, self-hosted cloud replacements (Vaultwarden, Pi-hole/NextDNS, Headscale, Syncthing, CryptPad), Tails OS, Qubes OS, and GrapheneOS.
  • Threat Intelligence & Cognitive Security: Dissecting commercial spyware reports from The Citizen Lab (UofT) and collaborating with the Stanford Cognitive Security Task Force on AI persuasion, information integrity, and neurotech/BCI privacy.
  • Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT) & Investigative Data: Tracking Bellingcat investigative toolkits, cross-border public-interest reporting from OCCRP, and open-source data journalism tools & webinars from ProPublica (Nonprofit Explorer & github.com/propublica).
  • Hacker Pedagogy & Open Web Spirit: Screening Chaos Computer Club (CCC) lectures and running participatory sessions in the spirit of MozFest, Aaron Swartz Day (with SecureDrop & the Internet Archive), the RSA Conference city-wide key-signing party, and the DEF CON Biohacking Village.
  • Public-Interest Bandwidth: Advocating for and operating Tor Snowflake proxies, obfs4 bridges, and non-profit relay partnerships (Emerald Onion & Nos Oignons).

how to get involved

We meet weekly on Thursdays from 4:00–5:30 PM at Frontier Tower for hands-on clinics, and tune into regular speaker series hosted by Bellingcat, EFF, and The Citizen Lab:

Cadence Event / Series Where & Focus
Weekly — Thu 4:00–5:30 PM Frontier Tower Cypherpunk Clinic Hands-on EFF Surveillance Self-Defense & Tails OS USB flashing clinic, Tor Snowflake/bridge setup, PGP key signing, CCC screenings, and Stanford CSTF / spyware briefings at Frontier Tower, SF.
Biweekly Thursdays Bellingcat Discord Stage Talks Live OSINT panels, tool walkthroughs, and Q&As hosted on Bellingcat's official Discord (plus OCCRP & ProPublica investigative webinars).
Recurring EFF Events & EFFecting Change EFF's livestream speaker series (archived on the Internet Archive), Bay Area EFF meetups, and Electronic Frontier Alliance (EFA) sessions.
Recurring Citizen Lab Lectures & Briefings Public webinars, Munk School seminars, and mercenary spyware disclosures from The Citizen Lab at UofT.
