what we do

Privacy and cognitive autonomy require active defense and shared infrastructure. Born in the San Francisco Bay Area in 1992 out of The WELL, Community Memory, and toad.com—and inspired by interdisciplinary spaces like Paris's La Paillasse and La Quadrature du Net (π)—the Cypherpunk Society equips builders, researchers, and investigative minds through hands-on practice:

  • Surveillance Self-Defense & Freedom OS: Monthly hands-on clinics running EFF's Surveillance Self-Defense curriculum, Tails OS USB verification & flashing, Qubes OS, and GrapheneOS.
  • Threat Intelligence & Cognitive Security: Dissecting commercial spyware reports from The Citizen Lab (UofT) and collaborating with the Stanford Cognitive Security Task Force on AI persuasion, information integrity, and neurotech/BCI privacy.
  • Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT): Tracking Bellingcat investigative toolkits and cross-border public-interest reporting from OCCRP.
  • Hacker Pedagogy & Open Web Spirit: Screening Chaos Computer Club (CCC) lectures and running participatory sessions in the spirit of MozFest, Aaron Swartz Day (with SecureDrop & the Internet Archive), the RSA Conference city-wide key-signing party, and the DEF CON Biohacking Village.
  • Public-Interest Bandwidth: Advocating for and operating Tor Snowflake proxies, obfs4 bridges, and non-profit relay partnerships (Emerald Onion & Nos Oignons).
